About Us

Shristi Art Academy has been a home for young artists since 1976. What began as a small neighbourhood drawing class has grown into one of the region's most trusted art academies, having personally guided over 12,000 students through their creative journey.

Our Journey
1976
Academy founded with a handful of students and a shared love for drawing.
1990s
Expanded curriculum to include painting, sketching, and exam-focused batches.
Today
Trusted by generations of families, with 12,000+ students trained to date.
