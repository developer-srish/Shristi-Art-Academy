About Shristi Art Academy

Shristi Art Academy is a creative learning space dedicated to helping students discover the joy of drawing, painting and artistic expression. Since 1976, the academy has focused on developing strong fundamentals while allowing every learner to grow at their own pace.

Our Story
1976 — The Beginning
The academy began with a simple vision: make quality drawing guidance accessible to students who love art.
Growing Through Generations
Drawing, sketching and painting became part of the creative journeys of students from different generations.
Expanding Skills
The learning experience grew to include colour work, perspective, shading, competition preparation and exam-focused practice.
Today
Shristi Art Academy continues to encourage students to learn patiently, practise consistently and create confidently.
Our Mission

Make Creativity A Skill For Life

We aim to create an environment where students can learn artistic fundamentals without losing their imagination. Every lesson is an opportunity to observe, practise, experiment and improve.

What We Believe

🎯 Fundamentals Matter

Strong foundations in shape, proportion, perspective, composition and shading make advanced artwork easier to understand.

🧠 Every Student Learns Differently

Students have different interests, speeds and strengths. Encouragement and patient guidance help each learner progress.

💪 Practice Builds Confidence

Consistent practice turns uncertainty into familiarity and helps students approach new subjects with confidence.

❤️ Art Should Be Enjoyable

A positive learning environment makes it easier for students to experiment, make mistakes and keep improving.

For Parents

Why Art Education Matters

Art gives children a space to observe, think, experiment and communicate ideas visually. Regular creative practice can also encourage patience, attention to detail and confidence in presenting original work.

Our Promise
